## Exact pre-mutation binding closes the stale-plan overwrite gap

`snapshotProtectedState` reads full documents for dealers, dealer and supplier opening-ledger rows, opening-stock movements, bank accounts, and cash accounts. Its `preMutationHashes` therefore include all dealer reset fields, both ledger `runningBalance` values, stock `balance`, bank `currentBalance`, and cash `currentBalance` plus `lastUpdated`. The complete snapshot is nested under `manifest.preserve`, binding those hashes into the dry-run fingerprint supplied to `--manifest`.

Before any write, `assertPlanUnchangedInTransaction` rebuilds that snapshot with the active session and compares every exact pre-mutation hash. A change to any overwritten input refuses the reset before the full-delete loop or any selective delete/update begins.

Post-reset verification uses the intended normalized view instead. Dealer derived fields and bank/cash normalized fields are omitted from protected-document hashes, opening-ledger hashes omit only `runningBalance`, and the opening-stock hash omits only `balance`. `verifyResetState` separately requires those fields to equal their reset formulas; it does not compare post-write `preMutationHashes` to the prior state.

## Voucher-derived journal partition and reconciliation lifecycle

`buildTransactionJournalFilter` deletes non-Opening Entry journals with approved dealer/supplier invoice, payment, sales-order, or purchase-order provenance, plus journals whose `referenceId` belongs to a voucher in the full-delete plan. The latter catches voucher journals created with `referenceType: Manual`. The retained `$nor` predicate is the exact non-opening complement, while Opening Entry journals are hashed separately.

Retained non-opening journals, Opening Entry journals, expenses, TDS entries, financial-year closings, year-end checklists, and completed legacy Opening Stock adjustments are content-hashed. `bankreconciliations` is now full-deleted and fingerprinted with vouchers, avoiding retained `clearedItems` entries keyed by deleted voucher IDs.

## GRN counters and journal-voucher numbering

The shared Counter model is used by GRN generation with `grn-YYYYMMDD` IDs, matching the case-insensitive `^grn-` deletion filter. Other counter rows are fully hashed and preserved. Reviewed transaction number generators otherwise derive their next values from collections that are fully deleted, so those namespaces restart without broader counter deletion.

JournalVoucher numbering does not use Counter. Automatic accounting/TDS creation selects the highest surviving monthly `JV-YYYYMM-*` number, and manual creation retains its existing count-based `JV-YYYY-*` fallback. The reset therefore preserves current-database uniqueness through surviving journal rows and the unique index, but it intentionally has no independent JV high-water mark and may reuse a number that belonged only to a deleted journal.
