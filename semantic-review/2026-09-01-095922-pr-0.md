# Selecting-actor permission replay for linked invoices

Linked invoice creation and item-bearing draft updates canonicalize submitted lines under the authenticated creator/editor and replace submitted permission snapshots with that actor’s database-backed identity and current level assignments. Approval derives the replay actor from persisted snapshots on selected-level lines rather than using the approver. Watch for: no confirmed high- or medium-severity defects in the scoped behavior.

**Verdict**: APPROVED

## High-level view

Creation and item updates share the canonicalization boundary that replaces permission snapshots under `req.user._id`. Updates without items leave existing snapshots unchanged because no level selection is resubmitted.

Approval separates approval authority from level ownership by replaying with the one actor persisted across selected-level lines. The actor and current assignments are reloaded inside the approval transaction, so a missing actor or revoked assignment fails closed.
