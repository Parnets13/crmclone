# Aggregate-ledger stock lifecycle with FIFO Pending-order readiness

The change makes movement direction and quantity the stock source of truth, moves physical reservation to explicit Sales Order confirmation, and turns arrival processing into an advisory FIFO readiness calculation for Pending orders. Deterministic movement identities, stock/order leases, and optimistic Sales Order revisions protect confirmation, delivery, cancellation, partial dispatch, expiry, GRN inspection, adjustments, transfers, opening stock, and invoice reversal. New CRM, split, remainder, and Sales Executive orders enter Pending, while active quantity-changing paths refresh the queue after commit. Two remaining read/expiry paths do not follow the same invariants.

**Watch for:** **confirmed (medium)** scheduled expiry ignores legacy outstanding reservation rows, and **confirmed (medium)** lightweight stock summaries can return stale quantities after backdated movements.

**Verdict**: NEEDS_CHANGES

## High-level view

Confirmation now creates line-addressed reservation movements and recovers a partially committed attempt by filling only missing reservations. Cancellation and rejection reconcile actual outstanding quantities, DELIVERY rows remain distinct from reservations, and changed stock-sensitive lifecycle paths take the Sales Order lease before stock-key leases. Scheduled expiry follows that locking order but recognizes only newly tagged reservation rows, allowing a legacy/interrupted Pending order with blocked stock to expire.

FIFO arrival processing writes only Pending, non-expired orders through an `updatedAt` compare-and-set, so stale refreshes cannot replace concurrent business edits. Arrival remains advisory; confirmation repeats FIFO readiness and aggregate-stock checks while holding the relevant leases.

GRN inspection/edit/delete share one lifecycle lease, quantity-changing paths refresh the Pending queue, transfer duplicates are collapsed, adjustment duplicate quantities are validated together, and unchanged frontend retries retain one request identity.

Most stock reads now aggregate IN minus OUT. The lightweight `summary=true` stock endpoint remains an exception and selects the latest stored balance, which is invalid after backdated movement insertion.

## Scheduled expiry misses legacy blocked stock

**Confirmed (medium):** `cron/salesOrderExpiration.js:55-76` matches only rows with `salesOrder` populated and `movementRole` equal to `RESERVATION` or `RESERVATION_RELEASE`. Legacy reservation rows supported by the changed lifecycle can instead be linked by `referenceNo` and identified by `Stock Blocked` / `Stock Unblocked` remarks; the interactive helper handles both forms at `controllers/salesOrderController.js:60-110`.

A Pending order left by an interrupted legacy confirmation can pass the cron guard, become Expired, and retain its OUT movement indefinitely. Manual and controller-driven expiry reject the same order, so behavior depends on which expiry runner acts first. Reuse the controller reconciliation in a shared service, matching by Sales Order id or order number and counting role-tagged plus legacy reservation/release rows.

## Backdated movements bypass aggregate stock in lightweight summaries

**Confirmed (medium):** `controllers/stockController.js:161-177` implements order/invoice `summary=true` mode by sorting movements and taking `$first: '$balance'`. Changed writers accept business dates and append a movement whose balance reflects aggregate stock after insertion; when that movement is backdated, a later-dated pre-existing row remains first with its old snapshot. The endpoint can then disagree with `StockMovementService.getCurrentStock`, product stock, Sales Executive product stock, reservation validation, and FIFO allocation.

Stock at 10 followed by a backdated OUT of 2 has authoritative stock 8, while the later pre-existing movement can still carry balance 10 and win the summary sort. Replace sort/first with a group sum that adds IN quantities and subtracts OUT quantities, preserving the warehouse filter, paging, and lookup stages.
