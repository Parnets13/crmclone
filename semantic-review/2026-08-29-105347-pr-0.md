# Sequential discount enforcement and immutable linked-invoice replay

The backend now finalizes sequential discounts as policy-governed Sales Order snapshots and replays those snapshots into linked Dealer Invoices. Approval and partial dispatch coordinate through writes to the same Sales Order, while linked approval reconstructs pricing from persisted source data rather than submitted or current live discount drivers. Stable source-line identities, recalculated partial-dispatch credit exposure, finalized family history, and GST-inclusive legacy migration cover the remaining lifecycle. Backend syntax checks passed for the changed sequential-discount controllers, models, and policy utility.

## High-level view

The shared policy validates ordered stages against mapping maxima, user permissions, combined and legacy limits, and the all-inclusive master cap. Persisted policy and permission snapshots insulate linked-invoice replay from later mapping or dealer changes.

Linked approval reads canonical dependencies through its transaction session and treats the Sales Order as authoritative for GST and every discount driver. Client-provided linked pricing cannot reprice the source order.

Approval takes a version-matched Sales Order claim; partial dispatch saves that same order inside `session.withTransaction`, re-reading state on retries and responding only after the transaction. Stable line IDs remove duplicate-product ambiguity, split quantities rebuild credit exposure, and migration reverses GST from GST-inclusive MRP.

## Sales Order as the lifecycle serialization boundary

Approval matches and increments the linked Sales Order's `__v`; partial dispatch saves the same aggregate in a retryable transaction. Concurrent approval, dispatch, or another split therefore conflicts on shared state rather than committing independent derivatives. Each retry re-fetches state, and the callback returns data for an HTTP response sent only after transaction completion.

## Source-authoritative linked invoice canonicalization

Canonicalization resolves the Sales Order, Product, User, and dealer through the approval session, then reconstructs lines from source GST, direct discount, dealer extra, ordered levels, promises, and policy/permission snapshots. Source-linked processing returns before live mapping lookup. `sourceSalesOrderLineId` handles repeated products, while ambiguous product-only fallback is rejected.

The linked-pricing trust boundary is fail-closed: submitted GST and discount drivers are rejected or overwritten, and snapshot-backed permissions and caps are revalidated during approval.

## Partial quantities retain canonical credit exposure

Partial dispatch keys quantities and reasons by stable line identity and rebuilds retained and remainder totals after the split. Per-line `creditAmount`, aggregate exposure, and over-limit metadata follow the resulting quantities, with Sales Order save-time calculation providing the canonical total boundary.

## GST-inclusive migration and finalized family history

Legacy migration treats `SalesOrder.unitPrice` as GST-inclusive MRP, removes its GST component, and then applies sequential stages without adding GST twice. Family history selects finalized invoice or eligible Sales Order events by `discountFinalizedAt`, excluding draft and mutable states.
