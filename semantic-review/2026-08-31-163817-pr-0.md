# Sales Order View lifecycle and status regression hardening

The change adds a detail-first Sales Order View with persisted Sales Type, invoice, stock, discount, and audit information, plus a protected status action shared with a stricter backend lifecycle. Follow-up fixes bring ready out-of-stock confirmation into the normal credit and reservation path, invalidate stale credit approval when total exposure grows, and make cancellation restoration account for partial-dispatch releases. The active-invoice predicate is consistent across Sales Order list, detail, credit, and partial-dispatch paths, but status mutation itself does not use it, and the new `In Transit` state is absent from invoice selection. Earlier discount, Stock Arrived, auto-split, and expiry fixes remain intact in the scoped paths.

Watch for: **confirmed (high)** an active-invoice Sales Order can be cancelled or rejected from the new View action without coordinating the invoice; **confirmed (medium)** `Processing → In Transit` makes the order disappear from the invoice Sales Order selector; **confirmed (medium, coverage)** no focused automated tests exercise the new lifecycle boundaries.

**Verdict**: NEEDS_CHANGES

## High-level view

The frontend and dedicated backend status maps match exactly, including `Processing → In Transit`, and cancellation/rejection require remarks on both sides. The older generic edit endpoint and unrestricted status dropdown remain a separate, pre-existing lifecycle bypass; they are not introduced by this View/status patch or counted as new findings.

A ready out-of-stock Pending order can enter only Confirmed. Confirmation recalculates canonical direct-plus-dealer-extra credit exposure, re-reads current stock for every warehouse line, creates normal reservation movements, and only then clears `isOutOfStock` and normalizes tracking. The inherited read-then-write, non-transactional reservation architecture remains a pre-existing risk rather than a new regression.

Credit confirmation compares current total exposure with the exposure captured by prior Super Admin approval. Larger exposure clears the stale approval without changing status or stock; equal or lower exposure retains it. Cancellation/rejection restores SALE `OUT - IN` per product and warehouse, so partial-dispatch releases reduce rather than suppress the remaining restoration.

List analytics, detail View, credit exclusion, and partial dispatch use the same active-invoice predicate. The new status action does not enforce that boundary, allowing Sales Order and invoice lifecycles to contradict each other. In Transit is now reachable through the canonical status map but remains excluded from the Dealer Invoice Sales Order query.

Create/edit discount canonicalization, Sales Type/stock auto-split terms, Pending expiry cancellation, linked-invoice pricing replay, and partial-dispatch remainder terms were not changed by the status follow-up. Backend parsing passed, but no focused regression suite exists and dashboard-wide ESLint is not clean enough to serve as a scoped gate.

<details><summary>Details</summary>

## Active invoices do not constrain the new View status action

This is **confirmed (high)** in `SalesOrderDashboard.jsx:7757-7799` and `salesOrderController.js:1427-1605`. View renders every mapped transition solely from the current Sales Order status, and `updateSalesOrderStatus` does not load `DealerInvoice`. A Confirmed, Processing, or In Transit order with an approved invoice can be changed to Cancelled or Rejected; the endpoint restores any still-reserved SALE quantity and persists the terminal Sales Order state while the invoice remains active.

The invoice cancellation path later sets its linked Sales Order back to Confirmed (`dealerInvoiceController.js:2402-2412`), so cancelling that invoice can revive a Sales Order separately cancelled from View. Apply the existing active-invoice predicate in the status endpoint and reject destructive transitions while such an invoice exists unless invoice cancellation/reversal is coordinated in the same transaction. The frontend should reflect the rule, but the backend must remain authoritative.

## In Transit falls out of invoice selection

This is **confirmed (medium)** in `salesOrderController.js:1453-1458`, `SalesOrderDashboard.jsx:42-47`, and `dealerInvoiceController.js:1015-1017`. The canonical lifecycle allows `Processing → In Transit`, while `getDealerSalesOrders` selects only Confirmed, Processing, Delivered, and Completed. An order available for invoice creation while Processing disappears after advancing to In Transit, then reappears at Delivered.

Include In Transit in the invoice-eligible set, or deliberately restrict invoice creation to a narrower documented state and make every status follow that rule. The current omission conflicts with both the lifecycle ordering and the endpoint's inclusion of Confirmed and Processing.

## Ready out-of-stock confirmation uses the normal reservation boundary

The follow-up closes the prior bypass at `salesOrderController.js:1511-1735`. Aggregate `ready` only admits the Pending-to-Confirmed attempt; current movement balances are checked before tracking is cleared. Credit is rebuilt from persisted canonical stages, reservation movements use the ordinary Confirmed path, and `isOutOfStock` is cleared only after reservation succeeds.

Status persistence and movement writes still lack one transaction, and reservation remains read-then-insert. These are **confirmed pre-existing architecture risks** present in the base branch's ordinary confirmation path, not new defects in this follow-up. The new branch shares that boundary instead of bypassing stock movement handling.

## Canonical exposure and quantity-aware restoration resolve the regressions

At confirmation, `creditAmount` is recalculated from direct and dealer-extra stages for every now-eligible ready line (`salesOrderController.js:1532-1603`). Approval survives only when recomputed `newOutstanding` does not exceed its stored approved exposure; otherwise approval is cleared, refreshed over-limit metadata is persisted, and confirmation stops before stock changes. Legacy approvals without a finite approved exposure fail closed.

Cancellation/rejection after partial dispatch groups SALE OUT and IN movements by product/warehouse and writes only the positive difference (`salesOrderController.js:1788-1876`). Repeated reductions accumulate releases, fully removed lines restore zero on later cancellation, and duplicate lines sharing a warehouse are handled as one quantity ledger. Partial-dispatch discount snapshots, credit terms, and the 15-day remainder expiry are unchanged.

## Focused lifecycle coverage is absent

This is **confirmed (medium, coverage)**: no backend test/spec references `updateSalesOrderStatus`, `partialDispatch`, `creditAmount`, out-of-stock confirmation, or In Transit. Add integration coverage for active-invoice destructive transitions, Processing-to-In-Transit invoice eligibility, ready out-of-stock success and stock-loss failure, larger/equal/lower approved exposure, and cancellation after one or more partial dispatches.

Backend `node --check` passed for the Sales Order, Dealer Invoice, Sales Order model, and stock-arrival modules. Targeted dashboard ESLint parsed the component but reported 30 errors and 5 warnings across the full file, outside this scoped behavioral analysis, so it did not provide a clean regression signal.

## Pre-existing generic status bypass is not a new finding

The edit modal still offers an unrestricted status dropdown and submits through generic `PUT /sales-orders/:id`; that endpoint does not enforce the new transition graph and has older stock rules. This is **confirmed but pre-existing** in both HEAD and the working tree (`SalesOrderDashboard.jsx:6012-6055`, `salesOrderController.js:2618-2835`). It is not counted against this change's verdict, but the dedicated map is not a system-wide invariant until the older path delegates to the same transition service.
