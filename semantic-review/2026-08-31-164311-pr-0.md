# Sales Order View lifecycle and follow-up regression fixes

The change adds a detail-first Sales Order View, persists and displays Sales Type, and introduces a protected frontend/backend status workflow. Follow-up fixes route ready out-of-stock confirmation through current stock and canonical credit checks, invalidate stale over-limit approval when exposure grows, and restore only the reservation quantity remaining after partial dispatch. The active-invoice definition is consistent wherever it is used, but destructive status changes do not consult it, and the newly reachable In Transit state remains absent from invoice selection. Discount canonicalization, auto-split terms, expiry handling, and linked-invoice pricing are not otherwise changed by the status follow-up.

Watch for: **confirmed (high)** Cancelled/Rejected can be applied while an active non-draft invoice remains live; **confirmed (medium)** Processing → In Transit removes an order from the invoice Sales Order selector; **confirmed (medium, coverage)** no focused automated tests exercise these lifecycle boundaries.

**Verdict**: NEEDS_CHANGES

## High-level view

The View action and `updateSalesOrderStatus` use the same transition graph, including Processing → In Transit, and both require remarks for cancellation and rejection. The older generic edit endpoint remains a **confirmed pre-existing architecture risk** because it does not consistently use this graph.

List analytics, detail responses, credit exposure, and partial dispatch all define an active invoice as non-draft with status outside Cancelled, Rejected, and Draft. Status mutation omits that predicate, allowing a terminal Sales Order while its invoice remains active.

Ready out-of-stock confirmation requires the tracked aggregate to be ready, then independently checks current movement balances. It recalculates direct-plus-dealer-extra credit exposure, reserves through the ordinary Confirmed path, and clears out-of-stock tracking only after reservation succeeds.

Confirmation retains prior approval only when recomputed total exposure does not exceed the approved exposure. Partial dispatch recalculates retained and remainder exposure, while later cancellation/rejection restores `OUT - IN` per product and warehouse so earlier releases are deducted.

The status follow-up does not alter create/edit discount replay, Sales Type auto-split credit terms, Pending expiry behavior, partial-dispatch remainder terms, or linked-invoice discount replay. Invoice flow is affected at the two lifecycle boundaries above.

<details><summary>Details</summary>

## Active invoices do not constrain destructive View transitions

This is **confirmed (high)** in `crm_backend/controllers/salesOrderController.js:1435-1469` and `src/Sales&Purchase/SalesOrderDashboard.jsx:3104-3140`. The status handler loads no `DealerInvoice` model and accepts Cancelled or Rejected from the transition graph and remarks check; View offers those transitions even when refreshed detail reports `hasInvoice: true`.

The predicate is otherwise consistent: credit exposure, list analytics, detail lookup, and partial dispatch all exclude Cancelled, Rejected, Draft, and `isDraft: true` invoices (`salesOrderController.js:579-581`, `860-862`, `928-930`, `4793-4795`). Apply it to destructive status changes server-side, rejecting them or coordinating the invoice transition transactionally.

## In Transit falls outside invoice selection

This is **confirmed (medium)** in `salesOrderController.js:1452-1457`, `SalesOrderDashboard.jsx:39-45`, and `dealerInvoiceController.js:1014-1018`. Both transition maps allow `Processing → In Transit`, while `getDealerSalesOrders` selects Confirmed, Processing, Delivered, and Completed. The order disappears from invoice selection at In Transit and reappears at Delivered.

Add In Transit, or replace the selector with a deliberately narrower rule that does not include an earlier state while excluding its successor.

## Ready out-of-stock confirmation shares the normal reservation boundary

The ready aggregate only admits the attempt (`salesOrderController.js:1520-1531`). Confirmation rebuilds canonical credit exposure, re-reads every line's latest movement balance, returns before movement creation on shortage, and clears `isOutOfStock` and waiting/partial tracking only after Stock Blocked movements succeed (`salesOrderController.js:1536-1733`, `1890-1911`).

The balance check and reservation writes remain non-transactional read-then-insert operations. This is a **confirmed pre-existing architecture risk** in the base branch's ordinary confirmation path, not a new follow-up defect.

## Canonical exposure and quantity-aware restoration resolve the regressions

Confirmation rebuilds `creditAmount` from persisted direct and dealer-extra stages and retains approval only when recomputed `newOutstanding` is within the previously approved exposure (`salesOrderController.js:1536-1589`). Missing historical exposure fails closed; edit resets approved over-limit orders to Pending when canonical order exposure grows, and a partial-dispatch remainder receives fresh unapproved metadata (`salesOrderController.js:2400-2674`, `4969-5109`).

Cancellation/rejection groups SALE OUT and IN movements by product and warehouse and restores only the positive difference (`salesOrderController.js:1802-1860`). Because partial dispatch writes IN for every reduced quantity before shrinking the line (`salesOrderController.js:4890-4957`), cancellation restores only the retained reservation. Status persistence and these movement writes remain a **confirmed pre-existing architecture risk** because they are not one transaction.

## Scoped regression surface lacks automated coverage

Create and auto-split still canonicalize discounts before totals and credit; edit replays persisted policy unless discount drivers change; partial-dispatch remainders preserve Sales Type, credit days, snapshots, and 15-day expiry. Pending exit still cancels expiry, and linked invoices still replay the Sales Order policy snapshot.

This is **confirmed (medium, coverage)**: no test/spec file references the dedicated handler, ready out-of-stock confirmation, partial-dispatch restoration, In Transit, or credit-overlimit lifecycle. Add integration tests for active-invoice rejection, In Transit eligibility, ready confirmation with and without current stock, larger/equal/lower approved exposure, and cancellation after partial dispatch.

Backend syntax checks passed for the Sales Order controller, Dealer Invoice controller, Sales Order model, and stock-arrival service. Dashboard ESLint parsed but reported 30 errors and 5 warnings across the existing large component, so it is not a clean scoped gate.
