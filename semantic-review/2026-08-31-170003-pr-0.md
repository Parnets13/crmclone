# Sales Order lifecycle follow-up closes invoice, stock, and credit regressions

The follow-up blocks destructive Sales Order transitions when an active invoice exists, keeps In Transit orders invoice-eligible, and sends ready out-of-stock confirmation through normal reservation handling. Canonical credit exposure now supports pre-snapshot lines without relaxing current snapshot caps, while cancellation after partial dispatch restores only the reservation that remains. No confirmed defect remains in the five requested fixes. Non-transactional stock/status writes and the older generic edit path are unchanged baseline risks, not follow-up regressions.

Watch for: no remaining confirmed defects in the five re-reviewed behaviors.

**Verdict**: APPROVED

## High-level view

Active non-draft invoices use the same predicate in list/detail state and the server mutation gate. View removes Cancelled and Rejected when `hasInvoice` is true, while the backend independently rejects those transitions.

The dealer-invoice Sales Order query now includes In Transit alongside the other invoice-eligible lifecycle states.

Ready out-of-stock confirmation rechecks current movement balances, writes normal reservation movements, and clears out-of-stock tracking only after reservation succeeds. A shortage exits before either reservation or cleanup.

Current credit snapshots contribute direct and dealer-extra stages under their persisted master cap. Lines without ordered snapshots reconstruct those stages from persisted legacy fields under a neutral 100% ceiling, avoiding a missing-cap failure only for the legacy branch.

Cancellation/rejection restores SALE `OUT - IN` by product and warehouse. Partial-dispatch releases therefore reduce the later restoration rather than suppressing it or restoring the original quantity twice.

## Active invoices constrain destructive transitions at both boundaries

`getSalesOrder` and list analytics mark invoices active when they are non-draft and outside Cancelled, Rejected, and Draft. The View transition helper filters Cancelled and Rejected from that marker, and `updateSalesOrderStatus` repeats the database predicate before any warehouse, stock, or status mutation. A stale UI cannot bypass the authoritative server check.

## In Transit stays available to invoice creation

`getDealerSalesOrders` now selects Confirmed, Processing, In Transit, Delivered, and Completed. Processing → In Transit no longer removes an order from invoice selection.

## Ready out-of-stock orders cross the normal reservation boundary

The ready aggregate only admits Pending → Confirmed. Confirmation rebuilds credit exposure, checks each line against the latest movement balance, writes Stock Blocked movements, and then clears `isOutOfStock`, stock validation, and waiting/partial tracking.

The movement writes and final Sales Order save remain separate. This is a **confirmed pre-existing risk** shared by ordinary confirmation on `main`, not a defect introduced or left unique to the follow-up.

## Legacy credit reconstruction preserves current cap validation

`calculateCanonicalCreditLineAmount` filters current ordered snapshots to direct and dealer-extra stages and passes their `masterDiscountCap` into `calculateDiscountLine`; missing or exceeded current caps still fail closed. Without ordered stages, it reconstructs direct discount from `appliedDiscount.directDiscountPercentage` and dealer extra from `dealerExtraDiscount`, with the neutral cap limited to that legacy branch.

A calculation probe produced 855 from sequential 10% and 5% legacy stages on 1000 and rejected current 15% plus 10% stages under a 20% snapshot cap.

## Cancellation subtracts partial-dispatch releases

Partial dispatch records each reduced quantity as a SALE IN movement. Cancellation/rejection groups SALE movements by product and warehouse and restores `max(0, OUT - IN)`, covering repeated reductions, fully removed lines, and duplicate lines sharing the same stock key.

Cancellation movements and final status persistence remain non-transactional, another **confirmed pre-existing risk** rather than an unresolved quantity-accounting regression.

## Scope and validation boundary

The generic `PUT` edit flow remains a **confirmed pre-existing risk** because it does not uniformly delegate to the protected status handler. The reviewed View action and dedicated PATCH endpoint enforce the new rules; this follow-up does not make the lifecycle a system-wide service.

Parser checks passed for the Sales Order controller, Dealer Invoice controller, and sequential discount utility. No focused automated tests were found. Dashboard ESLint parsed the file but retained the baseline 30 errors and 5 warnings, including the disabled legacy-modal expression already present on `main`, so it was not a clean scoped gate.
