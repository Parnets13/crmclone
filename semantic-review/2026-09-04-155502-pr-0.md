# Reservation-aware sales-order expiry and signed stock summaries

The expiry cron now blocks automatic expiry when current role-tagged or legacy remark-tagged stock reservations remain, associating movements through either the Sales Order id or order number. The lightweight stock summary now derives each product/warehouse balance from signed movement quantities instead of the latest stored balance snapshot. Warehouse scoping and page-completion semantics remain unchanged. **Watch for:** No concrete high/medium correctness issue remains in the two targeted fixes or their direct effects.

**Verdict**: APPROVED

## High-level view

Sales-order expiry nets SALE reservation and release movements per product/warehouse. The lookup covers `salesOrder` and `referenceNo`, current `RESERVATION`/`RESERVATION_RELEASE` roles, and legacy `Stock Blocked`, `Stock Unblocked`, and `Stock Fully Unblocked` remarks.

Stock summary aggregation retains its optional warehouse match and deterministic product/warehouse pagination. Only `netStock` changes: it is now total IN quantity minus total OUT quantity, so backdated movements cannot leave the summary on a stale balance snapshot.

## Outstanding reservations gate automatic expiry

The lookup matches the reservation writers in `controllers/salesOrderController.js`: reservation rows are SALE/OUT movements and release rows are SALE/IN movements, with current rows carrying roles and legacy rows identified by established remarks. Grouping by product and warehouse prevents a release for one stock key from clearing another key’s reservation; any group with more blocked than released quantity prevents expiry.

## Signed totals preserve stock-summary scoping

`StockMovement.type` is constrained to IN or OUT and quantity is numeric, making the grouped signed sum the movement-ledger total. The warehouse `$match` remains before grouping, while product/warehouse sorting, page skip, `limit + 1`, slicing, and `hasNextPage` remain unchanged for the frontend’s page-until-complete flow.

## Narrow validation

Both modules pass `node --check`; `git diff --check main` reports no errors for the targeted files.
