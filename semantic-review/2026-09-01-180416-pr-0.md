# Invoice-only post-discount Dealer Invoice price increase

The change adds an exceptional percentage increase after all sequential discount stages while retaining the original discount amount and effective percentage for history. Server canonicalization recalculates the increase, embedded GST, invoice total, and Dealer Ledger debit for linked and standalone lines; the requested ₹34,962.75 + 10% example produces ₹3,496.28 and ₹38,459.03. The customer print uses the adjusted persisted total without rendering the internal percentage, amount, reason, actor, or timestamp. Two blocking controls remain: an approved Sales Order can bypass credit validation for the added exposure, and standalone line identity can retain another product’s audit actor/time. Watch for: **confirmed** increased linked invoices can exceed the exposure covered by Sales Order over-limit approval; **confirmed** a standalone draft update can move an unchanged percentage to a different product while preserving the prior product’s `AppliedBy`/`AppliedAt`.

**Verdict**: NEEDS_CHANGES

## High-level view

The increase helper receives the canonical post-discount amount, rounds the increase and final amount to paise, reverse-calculates embedded GST, and caps the final against gross MRP. Create, draft update/sync, linked approval replay, and model pre-save converge on those amounts while keeping discount history pre-increase.

The adjusted invoice total feeds notifications, Dealer Ledger invoice value, debit, running balance, and receivables. **Confirmed:** the linked approval path treats any Sales Order over-limit approval as authority to skip credit validation, without comparing the increased invoice exposure with the approved `newOutstanding`.

Controller canonicalization replaces client-supplied actor/time. **Confirmed:** standalone matching trusts a submitted subdocument `_id` without checking product identity, allowing a replacement product with the same percentage to inherit the old line’s actor and timestamp.

The customer print derives taxable amount and GST from the adjusted canonical total and suppresses internal increase metadata and discount presentation on affected lines. Legacy lines retain persisted totals, and status-only saves do not trigger item repricing.

<details><summary>Details</summary>

## Sales Order approval does not cover the invoice-only exposure increase

This is **confirmed** in `crm_backend/controllers/dealerInvoiceController.js:2239-2297` and `crm_backend/models/SalesOrder.js:354-373`. A Sales Order over-limit record stores the approved `orderAmount` and `newOutstanding`. Invoice approval sets `hasOrderApproval` from `isOverlimit` plus `approvedBy` and skips the complete credit-limit calculation without comparing adjusted `invoice.totalAmount` with that exposure.

Because the increase is invoice-only, the Sales Order approval does not include it. The higher amount can be committed to `invoiceValue`, `debitAmount`, and `runningBalance` without renewed approval. Retain prior approval only when recomputed outstanding is no greater than the approved `newOutstanding`; otherwise require a new over-limit decision before posting the invoice ledger entry.

## Standalone product replacement can inherit another line’s increase audit

This is **confirmed** in `crm_backend/controllers/dealerInvoiceController.js:59-79`, `:121-134`, and `:317-320`. `findExistingInvoiceItem` returns a submitted `sourceSalesOrderLineId` or `_id` match without comparing product identity. `canonicalizeOneTimePriceIncrease` then preserves that line’s `oneTimePriceIncreaseAppliedBy` and `oneTimePriceIncreaseAppliedAt` whenever the percentage is unchanged.

A standalone updater can submit an increased line’s `_id`, replace its product, and keep the percentage. The server recalculates the new product’s charge but attributes it to the previous product’s actor/time. Linked lines receive a later Sales Order source/product check; standalone lines do not. Require both line and product identity before preserving audit, or treat the increase as newly applied.

</details>
