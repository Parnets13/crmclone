# Strict combined-level cap enforcement and Sales Executive cart policy refresh

The follow-up fixes separate the arithmetic cap on selected discount levels from the reducing-basis master cap while preserving legacy mapping and cart compatibility. Non-applicable mapping caps may remain null, but level-capable sales mappings still require a valid combined cap. Sales Executive recalculation replaces stored cart policy with the authoritative API response and persists it. No actionable release blocker remains in the reviewed scope.

Watch for: No remaining actionable release blockers were found. Sales Executive request-to-Sales Order conversion intentionally reprices against current authoritative policy, and both caps remain enforced in that workflow.

**Verdict**: APPROVED

## High-level view

DiscountMapping accepts null for non-applicable cap fields, while controller and save-time checks conditionally require the master cap for sales and the combined-level cap for level-capable sales. Direct-sales and purchase null-cap cases therefore save without weakening level-based sales validation.

The shared policy compares the six-decimal arithmetic level sum to the combined cap with only a floating-point epsilon. A total of 10 passes a cap of 10; 10.005 is rejected.

The Sales Executive calculation response refreshes policy fields in React state and AsyncStorage. Local validation falls back from `combinedLevelDiscountCap` to legacy `maxDiscountPercentage`, and final submission is independently repriced and cap-validated by the backend.

## Conditional DiscountMapping caps

A targeted schema and pre-save hook probe confirmed direct sales with a valid master cap and null combined cap succeeds, purchase with both caps null succeeds, level-based sales with a valid combined cap succeeds, and level-based sales with a null combined cap fails. Create, update, approval, and save-time checks preserve these conditional requirements, including the legacy mapping fallback.

## Strict arithmetic boundary

A runtime probe against `validateDiscountPolicy` confirmed 10 against cap 10 is valid and 10.005 is rejected with `COMBINED_LEVEL_DISCOUNT_CAP_EXCEEDED`. Sales Executive preview and request submission use the same shared assertion, so skipping mobile validation cannot bypass the arithmetic cap; direct and dealer-extra stages remain covered by the separate effective master cap.

## Refreshed Sales Executive policy persistence

After successful recalculation, `OrderCartScreen` persists the returned mapping identity, direct discount, both caps, available levels, and dealer-extra discount on each cart line. Legacy cart lines resolve the combined cap as `combinedLevelDiscountCap ?? maxDiscountPercentage ?? null` during display, editing, and final local validation, while backend mapping lookup applies the corresponding legacy fallback.
